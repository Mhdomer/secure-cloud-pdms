## Citations and References

### Does (Author, Year) mean you must quote their exact words?

**No.** There are two distinct citation types:

**Paraphrase (preferred in engineering/technical writing):**
Write the idea in your own words — syntax can be completely different from the source.
> The lack of backup and recovery planning is a consistent factor in ransomware cases that caused prolonged disruption (Argaw et al., 2019).

**Direct quote (use sparingly):**
Copy exact words — must use quotation marks + citation + page number.
> "AWS manages security *of* the cloud, you are responsible for security *in* the cloud." (AWS, 2023)

**Rule:** If the idea, finding, or claim came from someone else — cite them, regardless of whether your wording matches theirs. You never need to copy their exact words to cite them. Paraphrasing is preferred.
