# Appendix Requirements

Every appendix must have a title. Appendices appear after the References section.

---

| Appendix | Title | Description | Status |
|----------|-------|-------------|--------|
| Appendix A | Project Activity Schedule | Gantt Chart covering ALL PSM1 activities within the semester + projected PSM2 plan. **One Gantt Chart per project, per page.** | ⬜ |

## Gantt Chart — What It Must Cover

The Gantt Chart for Appendix A must show:

**PSM1 (completed):**
- Sprint 1: Requirements and Architecture Design
- Chapter 1 writing
- Chapter 2 writing
- Chapter 3 writing
- Chapter 4 writing
- Progress 1 submission (9 May 2026)
- Diagram production (Figures 2.1–4.8)
- Front matter (abstract)
- Final submission preparation

**PSM2 (projected):**
- Sprint 2: Network Infrastructure and Database Layer
- Sprint 3: Application Layer and Authentication
- Sprint 4: DevSecOps Pipeline and Monitoring
- Sprint 5: Security Evaluation and Compliance Testing
- Chapter 5 writing (Implementation and Testing)
- Chapter 6 writing (Conclusion)
- Final report submission

> 📎 **ATTACH:** `Appendix A` — Gantt Chart. Use Excel, Google Sheets, or draw.io. One page. Rows = tasks, Columns = weeks across both semesters. Shade PSM1 weeks in one colour, PSM2 weeks in another. Mark key milestones (Progress 1, Final Submission).

---

## Appendix Numbering Convention

Label appendices alphabetically: Appendix A, Appendix B, Appendix C, etc.  
Each must have a title on its own page before the content, e.g.:

```
APPENDIX A

PROJECT ACTIVITY SCHEDULE
```
